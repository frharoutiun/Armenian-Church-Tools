About this text. The Krapar (Classical Armenian) text here is the Sharagnots of the Armenian Church itself, not a translation - taken from the edition Abp. Eprem Tabakian published, in which he placed his own Western Armenian translation of the Sharagnots alongside the original Krapar. This tool uses only the original Krapar text; his Western Armenian translation is not reproduced here.

About this site. Armenian text is the corrected-spelling (dictionary-form) pass of the cleaned Krapar corpus. "Ganon" and other transliterations follow the parish's Maroon Book convention (via TR). Root/declension search is powered by uniparser-classical-armenian, which analyzes every unique word form in the corpus against its dictionary root. Hymn-type genres follow Fr. Daniel Findikyan's "Armenian Hymnography" (St. Nersess Armenian Seminary, LS323). English translations are being added section by section. Questions or corrections: frharoutiun@gmail.com.
