The Krapar Sharagan corpus of Abp. Eprem Tabakian's Western Armenian Sharagnots, for reading, browsing, and search.

Search the Corpus

Armenian search matches every case/number ending of a word's dictionary root, not just the exact spelling typed. English search currently covers only the one section fully translated so far.

Results

Armenian text is the corrected-spelling (dictionary-form) pass of the cleaned Krapar corpus. "Ganon" and other transliterations follow the parish's Maroon Book convention (via TR). Root/declension search is powered by uniparser-classical-armenian. Hymn-type genres follow Fr. Daniel Findikyan's "Armenian Hymnography" (St. Nersess Armenian Seminary, LS323).
